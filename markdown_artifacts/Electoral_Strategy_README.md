## 📊 GAME OVERVIEW

### Core Mechanics
- **Players**: 2-4
- **Goal**: Win 270+ electoral votes through the Electoral College
- **Turns**: 12 rounds representing the campaign season
- **Actions**: Play Policy and Campaign cards to shift state lean
- **Events**: Random events affect all players (with dice-based severity)
- **Debates**: Competitive showdowns at Rounds 4, 8, 12

### What Makes It Unique
✅ **Electoral College simulation** - spatial puzzle, you don't need to win everywhere
✅ **Light randomness** - dice only for events, debates, and 4 risky cards
✅ **Strategic depth** - balance base vs swing states, respond to events
✅ **Educational** - players learn how campaigns actually work
✅ **Replayable** - event variety and dice keep games fresh

### Playtime
- **Setup**: 5 minutes
- **Gameplay**: 60-90 minutes (12 rounds)
- **Cleanup**: 2 minutes

## 🤝 PLAYTESTING FEEDBACK GUIDE

When playtesting, track:

### Balance Questions
- [ ] Can both "sides" (liberal/conservative strategies) win?
- [ ] Are swing states appropriately valuable?
- [ ] Is any card overpowered or useless?
- [ ] Does the dice randomness feel fair?

### Engagement Questions
- [ ] Do players feel engaged every turn?
- [ ] Are decisions meaningful?
- [ ] Is there enough interaction between players?
- [ ] Does the game end at the right time (too long/short)?

### Clarity Questions
- [ ] Do players understand how to win?
- [ ] Are card effects clear?
- [ ] Is the lean tracking system intuitive?
- [ ] Do players know when to roll dice?

### Fun Factor
- [ ] Are there memorable moments?
- [ ] Do players want to play again?
- [ ] Does the theme come through?
- [ ] Is there a good balance of strategy and luck?
