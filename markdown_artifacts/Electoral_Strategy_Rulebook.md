# ELECTORAL STRATEGY
## Campaign for the Presidency - Official Rulebook

**Players**: 2-4
**Ages**: 14+
**Playing Time**: 60-90 minutes
**Complexity**: Medium

---

## GAME OVERVIEW

In Electoral Strategy, you lead a presidential campaign competing to win 270 electoral votes through the Electoral College system. You'll craft policy positions, run strategic campaigns, and navigate unpredictable national events. Every decision matters—appeal to swing voters without alienating your base, respond to crises, and outmaneuver your opponents to reach the White House.

---

## COMPONENTS

### Cards (85 total)
- **30 Policy Cards** - Your platform positions (Healthcare, Economy, Energy, Immigration, Education, Social, Military, Technology)
- **20 Campaign Cards** - Tactical moves (Ground Game, Media, Strategy, Fundraising)
- **25 Event Cards** - Global events affecting all players (Economic, Foreign Affairs, Domestic Crisis, Social, Technology)
- **4 Running Mate Cards** - Choose your VP (each player selects 1)
- **3 Debate Cards** - Special showdown cards
- **3 Wild Cards** - Game-changing moments

### Game Board
- **Electoral Map** - United States map showing all 50 states + DC
- **Electoral Vote Count** - Each state shows electoral votes (538 total)
- **State Lean Tracker** - Sliding scale for each state (-15 Red to +15 Blue)

### Tokens & Markers
- **State Lean Markers** - Track current lean for each state
- **Rally Markers** - Persistent ground game presence
- **Campaign Point Tokens** - Your action currency
- **Round Tracker** - Track game progress (Rounds 1-12)
- **Active Event Cards Board** - Display ongoing events

### Dice
- **2× six-sided dice (d6)** - For event severity and risky plays

## SETUP

### 1. Board Setup
1. Place the Electoral Map in the center of the table
2. Set all State Lean Markers to their **starting positions**:
   - **Safe Blue States** (lean +10 to +15): CA, NY, MA, VT, IL, WA, OR, HI, CT, MD, DE, RI, NJ
   - **Safe Red States** (lean -10 to -15): TX, AL, MS, WV, OK, WY, ID, UT, ND, SD, NE, KS, TN, KY, AR, LA, IN, MO, SC, AK
   - **Lean Blue States** (lean +5 to +9): VA, CO, NM, MN, NH, ME
   - **Lean Red States** (lean -5 to -9): FL, OH, IA, MT
   - **Swing States** (lean 0 to ±4): PA, MI, WI, AZ, GA, NC, NV
3. Place Round Tracker on Round 1
4. Set aside the Active Events board

### 2. Deck Preparation

**Player Decks** (one for each player):
1. Separate Policy and Campaign cards
2. Shuffle together: 30 Policy + 16 Campaign cards (remove 4 Fundraising cards)
3. Set aside: 4 Fundraising cards (players can play these from a shared pool)
4. Set aside: 3 Wild Cards (shuffled into deck at Round 6)

**Event Deck**:
1. Shuffle all 25 Event cards
2. Place face-down near the board

**Debate Cards**:
1. Set aside - dealt during Debate Rounds (Rounds 4, 8, 12)

### 3. Player Setup

Each player:
1. **Choose a Running Mate** - Select 1 Running Mate card (permanent effects apply immediately)
2. **Draw starting hand** - Draw 7 cards from your deck
3. **Receive Campaign Points** - Start with 5 Campaign Points

### 4. Determine Starting Player
- Roll dice - highest roll goes first
- Play proceeds clockwise

## HOW TO WIN

**Victory Condition**: Be the first to reach **270+ electoral votes** by winning states

**How States Are Won**:
- When a state's lean marker reaches **±7 or more** in your direction, you control that state
- You gain that state's electoral votes toward your total
- States can flip throughout the game if lean shifts

**Game End**:
- **Immediate Win**: First player to 270+ electoral votes
- **OR After Round 12**: Highest electoral vote count wins
- **Tiebreaker**: Most states controlled, then most swing states controlled

## TURN STRUCTURE

Each round consists of all players taking one turn in clockwise order.

### Phase 1: UPKEEP
1. **Advance Round Tracker** (first player only)
2. **Resolve Ongoing Events** - Apply effects from active Event cards
3. **Check Rally Markers** - Apply +1 lean from each active Rally marker
4. **Gain Campaign Points** - Receive 3 Campaign Points (base income)

### Phase 2: DRAW
1. Draw 2 cards from your deck
2. Hand limit: 10 cards (discard down to 10 if over)

### Phase 3: EVENT (Skip on Rounds 1-2)
1. **Draw 1 Event card** from Event deck
2. **Roll 1d6 for Event Severity**:
   - **1-2**: Minor Event (halve all numbers, -1 duration)
   - **3-4**: Standard Event (as written on card)
   - **5-6**: Major Event (1.5× all numbers, +1 duration)
3. Place Event card on Active Events board with duration marker
4. Apply immediate effects
5. At end of round, reduce all Event durations by 1 (remove when 0)

### Phase 4: ACTION
You may take **up to 3 actions** on your turn. Actions include:

**A. Play a Policy Card**
- Pay Campaign Point cost
- Apply Primary Effect (gain lean in listed states)
- Apply Backlash (lose lean in listed states)
- Apply any Special conditions

**B. Play a Campaign Card**
- Pay cost and resolve effect
- If card has 🎲 symbol, roll dice and consult card

**C. Play a Fundraising Card** (once per turn)
- Play from shared Fundraising pool
- Gain Campaign Points as listed
- Return to pool after use

**D. Play a Running Mate Ability** (once per game)
- Use your Running Mate's one-time special ability

**E. Pass**
- End your Action phase early
- Does not count as an action

### Phase 5: CLEANUP
1. Discard cards if over hand limit (10 cards)
2. Check for victory condition (270+ electoral votes)
3. Pass turn to next player

---

## SPECIAL ROUNDS

### DEBATE ROUNDS (Rounds 4, 8, 12)

On these rounds, **all players participate in a Debate Showdown**:

1. **Each player selects 1 Debate card** face-down from the 3 Debate cards
2. **All players reveal simultaneously**
3. **All players roll 1d6**
4. **Add roll result to Debate card's Base Value**:
   - Knockout Blow: Base 3
   - Steady Performance: Base 2
   - Gaffe Recovery: Base 1
5. **Highest total wins the debate**
   - Winner applies their "Effect if you win"
   - Losers apply their "Effect if you lose" (if any)
6. **Ties**: Re-roll until someone wins
7. Return Debate cards to the pool

**Example Debate:**
```
Player A: Knockout Blow (3) + rolls 5 = 8
Player B: Steady Performance (2) + rolls 6 = 8
TIE - Re-roll: Player A rolls 2 (total 5), Player B rolls 4 (total 6)
Player B wins!
```

### MID-GAME TWIST (Round 6)
- Shuffle the 3 Wild Cards into each player's deck
- These can now be drawn normally

### FINAL STRETCH (Rounds 10-12)
- Certain cards can only be played now (marked "Final rounds only")
- No new Event cards are drawn (existing events continue)
- All players gain +2 Campaign Points per turn (total of 5)

## CARD TYPES EXPLAINED

### 🎲 RISKY CARDS (Light Randomness)

Some Campaign cards require dice rolls:

**Viral Social Media Campaign 🎲**
- Roll 1d6 after playing:
  - 1-2: Flop (+1 lean only)
  - 3-5: Viral (+2 lean as written)
  - 6: Mega-viral (+3 lean + draw 1 card)

**Celebrity Endorsement 🎲**
- Roll 1d6 after playing:
  - 1-2: Backfires (-1 lean in rural areas)
  - 3-6: Success (+2 lean as planned)

**Attack Ad 🎲**
- Roll 1d6 after playing:
  - 1: Backfires on you (-1 lean in target state)
  - 2-5: Works (-2 opponent lean)
  - 6: Devastating (-3 opponent lean)

**Hail Mary 🎲** (Final 2 rounds only)
- Roll 1d6 after playing:
  - 1-2: +2 target, -3 elsewhere
  - 3-4: +3 target, -2 elsewhere
  - 5-6: +5 target, -1 elsewhere

### ⚡ EVENT CARDS (Always roll for severity)

All Event cards follow the Event Severity system:
- Draw the Event card
- Roll 1d6 immediately
- Apply modified effects based on roll
- Place on Active Events board with duration counter

### INSTANT CARDS

Some cards can be played **outside your turn**:
- **Crisis Management** - Block an Attack Ad or scandal on opponent's turn
- These don't count against your 3-action limit

### ONGOING EFFECTS

Some cards place markers:
- **Rally Markers** - Grant +1 lean per turn for duration
- **Voter Registration Drive** - Grant +1 lean per turn for 3 turns
- Track these with tokens on the state

## ADVANCED RULES

### CARD COMBOS
Some cards synergize when played together **in the same turn**:
- **Infrastructure Investment + Union Support** = +1 additional lean
- **Mental Health Initiative + Education card** = +1 bonus lean
- Check card text for "If X, then Y" conditions

### BLOCKING & COUNTERS
- **Crisis Management** blocks Attack Ads and scandals
- **Veterans Benefits** neutralizes "Military Scandal" event
- **Door-to-Door Campaign** makes state immune to Attack Ads this turn
- **Nuclear Energy Expansion** neutralizes "Energy Crisis" event

### STATE CONTROL THRESHOLDS

| Lean Range | Status | Electoral Votes |
|------------|--------|-----------------|
| -15 to -7 | Opponent controls | Opponent gets votes |
| -6 to +6 | Contested | No one gets votes |
| +7 to +15 | You control | You get votes |

### CAMPAIGN POINT ECONOMY

**Gaining Campaign Points:**
- +3 per turn (base income)
- +5 per turn in final stretch (Rounds 10-12)
- Fundraising cards (Major Donor: +4, Super PAC: +5, etc.)
- Email Fundraising Blast: +1 per state you lead

**Spending Campaign Points:**
- Policy cards: 1-3 points
- Campaign cards: 0-3 points
- No maximum - save points across turns

## STRATEGY TIPS

### Early Game (Rounds 1-4)
- **Secure your base** - Shore up states leaning your direction
- **Build Campaign Points** - Play fundraising cards
- **Plant Rally Markers** - Ground game pays off over time
- **Prepare for first Debate** - Save key cards

### Mid Game (Rounds 5-8)
- **Fight for swing states** - PA, MI, WI, AZ, GA, NC, NV are critical
- **Respond to Events** - Adapt strategy based on active events
- **Watch opponent's lead** - Use Attack Ads or Opposition Research
- **Wild Cards enter** - Game becomes more chaotic

### Late Game (Rounds 9-12)
- **Count to 270** - Focus only on states needed for victory
- **Use GOTV and Hail Mary** - High-risk, high-reward plays
- **Final Debate (Round 12)** - Can swing multiple states at once
- **Don't overextend** - Protect states you already control

### Key Tactics
✅ **Path to Victory** - Always know which states = 270 votes
✅ **Diminishing Returns** - Don't over-invest in won states
✅ **Event Adaptation** - Major events change everything
✅ **Hand Management** - Keep diverse card types
✅ **Timing** - Save powerful cards for when they matter most

## VARIANTS & OPTIONAL RULES

### 2-Player Competitive
- Standard rules
- More direct competition for swing states
- Consider using "Momentum Shift" wild card more strategically

### 3-4 Player Free-For-All
- Standard rules
- Coalition-building encouraged
- Attack Ads can target any opponent

### 3-4 Player Team Mode (2v2)
- Teams share electoral vote total
- Teammates sit opposite each other
- Team to 270+ wins
- Can't Attack Ad your teammate

### Historical Scenario Mode
- Start with actual 2020 or 2024 lean positions
- Include specific events from that election
- Try to recreate or change history

### Short Game (6 Rounds)
- Double all lean effects
- Skip Events in Rounds 1-2
- Debates at Rounds 2, 4, 6
- First to 270 or highest after Round 6

### Expert Mode: Polling Uncertainty
Add more randomness:
- At end of each round, roll 1d6 for each contested state (lean 0-6)
  - 1: State shifts -1 toward opponent
  - 2-5: No change
  - 6: State shifts +1 toward you
- Simulates polling errors

## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**Q: Can I play more than 3 actions if I have Campaign Points?**
A: No, you're limited to 3 actions per turn regardless of resources.

**Q: What happens if two players reach 270 on the same round?**
A: Finish the round. Highest electoral vote count wins. If tied, most states controlled wins.

**Q: Can I save cards from Debates?**
A: No, Debate cards are returned to the pool after each Debate round.

**Q: Do Event effects stack if multiple Events are active?**
A: Yes! If two Events both affect Healthcare cards, add all bonuses together.

**Q: Can I play a Policy card if I don't have enough Campaign Points?**
A: No, you must have sufficient points to pay the cost.

**Q: What if I run out of cards in my deck?**
A: Shuffle your discard pile to create a new deck.

**Q: Can states flip back and forth?**
A: Yes! Lean is dynamic. A state you control can be won back by an opponent.

**Q: Do I need exactly 270, or can I go over?**
A: You need 270 or more. Going over is fine and recommended for security.

**Q: Can I play the same Policy card twice if I draw another copy?**
A: Yes, but be aware of diminishing returns - each successive play on the same state has reduced impact.

## CREDITS & DESIGN NOTES

**Game Design**: Electoral Strategy Design Team
**Version**: 1.0
**Playtesting**: [Your playtest group]

**Design Philosophy**:
This game aims to simulate the strategic depth of presidential campaigns while remaining accessible and fun. The Light Randomness system ensures that skill matters most, but unpredictable events keep every game fresh. The Electoral College creates interesting spatial puzzles—you don't need to win everywhere, just the right places.

**Educational Value**:
Players will learn:
- How the Electoral College works
- Why swing states matter
- How campaigns balance base vs. swing voters
- The impact of national events on elections
- Strategic resource allocation

**Balance Notes**:
- Swing states (PA, MI, WI, AZ, GA, NC, NV) are intentionally easier to flip
- Safe states provide stability but require heavy investment to flip
- Events create asymmetric advantages that force adaptation
- Running Mate choice matters but doesn't dominate strategy
