# CAMPAIGN CARDS

## Ground Game (5 cards)

### Grassroots Rally
- **Type**: Campaign - Rally
- **Cost**: 2 Campaign Points
- **Effect**: Choose 1 state. +2 lean immediately. Place Rally marker (ongoing +1 per turn for 3 turns)
- **Limit**: Max 1 Rally marker per state
- **Flavor**: *"When we fight, we win!"*

### Town Hall Tour
- **Type**: Campaign - Engagement
- **Cost**: 2 Campaign Points
- **Effect**: Choose 1 region (3-5 connected states). +1 lean in each
- **Special**: Gain 1 bonus card draw next turn
- **Flavor**: *"Listening to the people, not the pundits."*

### Door-to-Door Campaign
- **Type**: Campaign - Ground Game
- **Cost**: 1 Campaign Point
- **Effect**: Choose 1 state. +1 lean. Immune to Attack Ads this turn
- **Flavor**: *"One conversation at a time."*

### Voter Registration Drive
- **Type**: Campaign - Ground Game
- **Cost**: 1 Campaign Point
- **Effect**: Choose 1 state. +1 lean per turn for next 3 turns
- **Special**: Stacks with Rally markers
- **Flavor**: *"Every voice matters."*

### Get Out The Vote (GOTV)
- **Type**: Campaign - Ground Game
- **Cost**: 2 Campaign Points
- **Effect**: Final 3 rounds only. Double all your bonuses in target state
- **Flavor**: *"This is what democracy looks like!"*

## Media & Advertising (6 cards)

### Positive Ad Campaign
- **Type**: Campaign - Media
- **Cost**: 1 Campaign Point
- **Effect**: +1 lean in up to 3 states of your choice
- **Flavor**: *"A message of hope and unity."*

### Attack Ad 🎲
- **Type**: Campaign - Media
- **Cost**: 2 Campaign Points
- **Effect**: Target opponent loses -2 lean in 1 state
- **Dice Roll Required**: Roll 1d6
  - **1**: Ad fact-checked, backfires on you (-1 lean in that state)
  - **2-5**: Works as intended (-2 opponent lean)
  - **6**: Devastating impact (-3 opponent lean)
- **Counter**: Can be blocked by "Crisis Management"
- **Flavor**: *"The people deserve to know the truth."*

### Viral Social Media Campaign 🎲
- **Type**: Campaign - Media
- **Cost**: 1 Campaign Point
- **Effect**: +2 lean in states with young voters (under 40)
- **Dice Roll Required**: Roll 1d6
  - **1-2**: Fails to go viral (only +1 lean)
  - **3-5**: Goes viral as planned (+2 lean)
  - **6**: Mega-viral! (+3 lean + draw 1 card)
- **Flavor**: *"Trending #1 nationwide... maybe."*

### Debate Performance
- **Type**: Campaign - Media
- **Cost**: 3 Campaign Points
- **Effect**: +2 lean in ALL swing states
- **Limit**: Can only be played during designated Debate rounds
- **Flavor**: *"That mic drop moment."*

### Presidential Endorsement
- **Type**: Campaign - Endorsement
- **Cost**: 2 Campaign Points
- **Effect**: +3 lean in 1 state, +1 lean in adjacent states
- **Limit**: Only 2 per deck
- **Flavor**: *"Standing on the shoulders of giants."*

### Celebrity Endorsement 🎲
- **Type**: Campaign - Endorsement
- **Cost**: 1 Campaign Point
- **Effect**: +2 lean in 1 urban/suburban state
- **Dice Roll Required**: Roll 1d6
  - **1-2**: Backfires! Celebrity says something controversial (-1 lean in rural areas)

- **Flavor**: *"Star power meets people power."*

# EVENT CARDS

**All Event cards require an Event Severity Roll when drawn**

**Roll 1d6 when drawing:**
- **1-2**: Minor Event (halve all numbers, -1 duration)
- **3-4**: Standard Event (as written)
- **5-6**: Major Event (1.5× all numbers, +1 duration)

## Debate Cards (Dealt during Debate rounds)

### Knockout Blow 🎯
- **Type**: Debate
- **Base Value**: 3
- **Effect if you win**: +3 lean in ALL swing states, opponent -2
- **Effect if you lose**: Your attack backfires, -2 lean in all swing states
- **Roll**: 1d6 and add to base value (competitive roll with opponent)
- **Flavor**: *"The moment everyone will remember."*

### Steady Performance 🎯
- **Type**: Debate
- **Base Value**: 2
- **Effect if you win**: +2 lean in all states where you're currently losing
- **Effect if you lose**: +1 lean in states where you're losing (still decent)
- **Roll**: 1d6 and add to base value
- **Flavor**: *"No gaffes, mission accomplished."*

### Gaffe Recovery 🎯
- **Type**: Debate
- **Base Value**: 1
- **Effect if you win**: Nullify opponent's effect + gain +1 lean nationally
- **Effect if you lose**: Opponent's card takes full effect
- **Roll**: 1d6 and add to base value
- **Special**: If opponent plays "Knockout Blow" and you win, their card backfires on them
- **Flavor**: *"Turning a stumble into a stride."*
