# ELECTORAL STRATEGY
## 🎲 Dice Reference Guide

**Quick reference for all dice rolling mechanics**

---

## WHEN TO ROLL DICE

### ⚡ 1. EVENT CARD SEVERITY (EVERY EVENT)

**When**: Immediately when any Event card is drawn
**Who Rolls**: The player who drew the card
**Dice**: 1d6

| Roll | Severity | Effect Modification |
|------|----------|---------------------|
| **1-2** | **MINOR EVENT** | • Halve all numerical effects (round down)<br>• Duration -1 round (minimum 1) |
| **3-4** | **STANDARD EVENT** | • Use card exactly as written<br>• No modifications |
| **5-6** | **MAJOR EVENT** | • Multiply all numerical effects by 1.5× (round up)<br>• Duration +1 round |

#### Examples:

**"Economic Recession" (Standard: -2 lean, 3 rounds)**
- Roll 1-2: Minor → -1 lean, 2 rounds
- Roll 3-4: Standard → -2 lean, 3 rounds
- Roll 5-6: Major → -3 lean, 4 rounds

**"Hurricane" (Standard: -3 lean in 1 state, 2 rounds)**
- Roll 1-2: Minor → -1 lean, 1 round
- Roll 3-4: Standard → -3 lean, 2 rounds
- Roll 5-6: Major → -5 lean + affects adjacent states, 3 rounds

**"Stock Market Boom" (Standard: +1 lean, 2 rounds)**
- Roll 1-2: Minor → +1 lean, 1 round (same effect, shorter)
- Roll 3-4: Standard → +1 lean, 2 rounds
- Roll 5-6: Major → +2 lean, 3 rounds

---

### 🎲 2. RISKY CAMPAIGN CARDS (4 Cards Total)

**When**: After playing the card, before applying effects
**Who Rolls**: The player who played the card
**Dice**: 1d6

---

#### Viral Social Media Campaign 🎲

**Base Cost**: 1 Campaign Point
**Target**: States with young voters (under 40)

| Roll | Result | Effect |
|------|--------|--------|
| **1-2** | **Flop** | Only +1 lean (instead of +2) |
| **3-5** | **Goes Viral** | +2 lean (as written) |
| **6** | **MEGA-VIRAL!** | +3 lean **+ draw 1 card** |

---

#### Celebrity Endorsement 🎲

**Base Cost**: 1 Campaign Point
**Target**: 1 urban or suburban state

| Roll | Result | Effect |
|------|--------|--------|
| **1-2** | **BACKFIRE!** | Celebrity controversy<br>-1 lean in rural areas<br>(no bonus in target state) |
| **3-6** | **Success** | +2 lean in target state |

---

#### Attack Ad 🎲

**Base Cost**: 2 Campaign Points
**Target**: 1 state (opponent loses lean)

| Roll | Result | Effect |
|------|--------|--------|
| **1** | **BACKFIRE!** | Ad gets fact-checked<br>**YOU** lose -1 lean in that state<br>(opponent unaffected) |
| **2-5** | **Works** | Opponent loses -2 lean |
| **6** | **DEVASTATING!** | Opponent loses -3 lean |

**Note**: Can be blocked by "Crisis Management" card

---

#### Hail Mary 🎲

**Base Cost**: 3 Campaign Points
**Timing**: **Final 2 rounds only** (Rounds 11-12)
**Target**: 1 state (all-in gamble)

| Roll | Result | Effect |
|------|--------|--------|
| **1-2** | **Desperation Shows** | +2 in target state<br>-3 lean in ALL other states |
| **3-4** | **Calculated Risk** | +3 in target state<br>-2 lean in ALL other states |
| **5-6** | **LIGHTNING IN A BOTTLE!** | +5 in target state<br>-1 lean in ALL other states |

**Strategic Note**: Only use when you MUST win one specific state for 270 votes!

---

### 🎯 3. DEBATE SHOWDOWNS (Rounds 4, 8, 12)

**When**: During designated Debate rounds
**Who Rolls**: ALL players participating in the debate
**Dice**: 1d6 (each player rolls)

#### How Debates Work:

1. **Select Debate Card** - Each player chooses 1 Debate card face-down
2. **Reveal Simultaneously** - All players flip at once
3. **Roll Dice** - Each player rolls 1d6
4. **Calculate Total** - Add die roll to Debate card's Base Value
5. **Determine Winner** - Highest total wins
6. **Apply Effects** - Winner applies their victory effect, losers apply their loss effect (if any)
7. **Handle Ties** - If tied, re-roll until one player wins

---

#### Debate Card Values:

| Debate Card | Base Value | Effect if You Win | Effect if You Lose |
|-------------|------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Knockout Blow** 🎯 | **3** | +3 lean in ALL swing states<br>Opponent -2 in swing states | Your attack backfires<br>-2 lean in all swing states |
| **Steady Performance** 🎯 | **2** | +2 lean in all states where you're currently losing | +1 lean in states where you're losing<br>(still decent outcome) |
| **Gaffe Recovery** 🎯 | **1** | Nullify opponent's effect<br>+1 lean nationally | Opponent's effect takes place<br>(no effect for you) |

---

#### Debate Examples:

**Example 1: Clear Winner**
```
Player A: Plays "Knockout Blow" (Base 3)
         Rolls: 4
         Total: 7

Player B: Plays "Steady Performance" (Base 2)
         Rolls: 5
         Total: 7

TIE! Re-roll:
Player A rolls 2 (new total: 5)
Player B rolls 6 (new total: 8)

WINNER: Player B
Effect: +2 lean in all states where Player B is losing
```

**Example 2: Gaffe Recovery Counter**
```
Player A: Plays "Knockout Blow" (Base 3)
         Rolls: 3
         Total: 6

Player B: Plays "Gaffe Recovery" (Base 1)
         Rolls: 6
         Total: 7

WINNER: Player B
Special: Because Player B played Gaffe Recovery and won against
         Knockout Blow, the attack backfires on Player A!
Effect: Player A loses -2 lean in swing states
        Player B gains +1 lean nationally
```

**Example 3: Both Play Knockout Blow**
```
Player A: Knockout Blow (3) + rolls 5 = 8
Player B: Knockout Blow (3) + rolls 4 = 7

WINNER: Player A
Effect: Player A: +3 lean in all swing states
        Player B: -2 lean in swing states (backfire penalty)
```

## DICE ROLLING RULES

### General Guidelines

✅ **Roll openly** - All dice rolls should be visible to all players
✅ **Roll on flat surface** - Ensure fair rolls
✅ **Reroll unclear results** - If die falls off table or lands cocked
✅ **Accept the outcome** - No "gimme" rerolls

### When Dice Are NOT Needed

❌ **Standard Policy Cards** - Always work exactly as written (no rolls)
❌ **Most Campaign Cards** - Only 4 cards require rolls (marked with 🎲)
❌ **Running Mate abilities** - Guaranteed effects
❌ **Fundraising cards** - Always gain listed Campaign Points

### Probability Reference

Understanding the odds helps strategic decision-making:

| Outcome | Probability |
|---------|-------------|
| Rolling exactly 6 | 16.7% (1 in 6) |
| Rolling 5-6 | 33.3% (1 in 3) |
| Rolling 4-6 | 50% (1 in 2) |
| Rolling 3-6 | 66.7% (2 in 3) |
| Rolling 1-2 | 33.3% (1 in 3) |

**Strategic Insight**:
- Celebrity Endorsement succeeds 66.7% of the time (rolls 3-6)
- Viral Social Media goes mega-viral only 16.7% (roll 6)
- Attack Ads backfire only 16.7% (roll 1)
- Major Events occur 33.3% of the time (rolls 5-6)

## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

❌ **Rolling for every card** - Only 4 Campaign cards + all Events + Debates need rolls
❌ **Forgetting Event severity** - ALWAYS roll when drawing Events
❌ **Miscalculating Major Events** - Remember to multiply by 1.5× AND add duration
❌ **Debate ties without reroll** - Always reroll ties until someone wins
❌ **Applying wrong dice table** - Each 🎲 card has its own unique table

## DICE VARIANT: NO RANDOMNESS MODE

If your group prefers **zero randomness**, use these alternatives:

**Events**:
- All Events are Standard severity (no roll)
- Or: Let the affected player choose Minor or Standard

**Risky Cards**:
- Viral Social Media: Always +2 lean
- Celebrity Endorsement: Always +2 lean (no backfire)
- Attack Ad: Always -2 opponent lean (no backfire or devastating)
- Hail Mary: Always +3 target, -2 elsewhere

**Debates**:
- Don't roll dice
- Compare only Base Values
- Ties: Both players apply "if you lose" effects

This creates a **pure strategy** game with no luck, but less excitement.

---

## TIPS FOR MANAGING DICE

**For Game Night:**
- Keep 2 different colored dice (one for each player in debates)
- Use a dice tray to prevent rolls going off-table
- Designate one player as "dice referee" for disputes

**For Accessibility:**
- Digital dice roller apps work fine
- Voice "calling" rolls before rolling reduces disputes
- Keep this reference sheet visible to all players

---

**Remember**: Dice add excitement, but skill and strategy still determine most outcomes!

The light randomness system ensures your decisions matter most, while creating memorable moments when a Mega-viral campaign or Major Event changes everything.

🎲 **Good luck, and may the odds be in your favor!** 🎲
