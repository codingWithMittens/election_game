# ELECTORAL STRATEGY
## Quick Reference Guide

*Print this double-sided and keep at the table during gameplay*

---

## TURN STRUCTURE

### 1️⃣ UPKEEP
- Advance Round Tracker (first player only)
- Resolve active Events
- Apply Rally markers (+1 lean each)
- **Gain 3 Campaign Points** (5 in Rounds 10-12)

### 2️⃣ DRAW
- Draw 2 cards
- Hand limit: 10 cards

### 3️⃣ EVENT (Skip Rounds 1-2)
- Draw 1 Event card
- **Roll 1d6 for severity**:
  - 1-2: Minor (half effects, -1 duration)
  - 3-4: Standard (as written)
  - 5-6: Major (1.5× effects, +1 duration)
- Place on Active Events board

### 4️⃣ ACTION (Take up to 3 actions)
- Play Policy Card (pay cost)
- Play Campaign Card (pay cost, roll 🎲 if needed)
- Play Fundraising Card (once per turn)
- Use Running Mate ability (once per game)
- Pass

### 5️⃣ CLEANUP
- Discard to hand limit (10)
- Check victory (270+ votes)
- Pass turn clockwise

---

## HOW TO WIN

🏆 **First to 270+ electoral votes wins**
- OR highest count after Round 12

### State Control
- **Your lean ≥ +7** → You control (gain electoral votes)
- **Lean -6 to +6** → Contested (no one controls)
- **Opponent lean ≥ +7** → Opponent controls

---

## DICE ROLLS

### ⚡ Event Severity (every Event card)
Roll 1d6:
- 1-2: Minor | 3-4: Standard | 5-6: Major

### 🎲 Risky Campaign Cards (4 cards)

**Viral Social Media:**
- 1-2: +1 lean | 3-5: +2 lean | 6: +3 lean + draw card

**Celebrity Endorsement:**
- 1-2: Backfire (-1 rural) | 3-6: Success (+2)

**Attack Ad:**
- 1: Backfire on you | 2-5: -2 opponent | 6: -3 opponent

**Hail Mary (Rounds 11-12 only):**
- 1-2: +2 target, -3 elsewhere
- 3-4: +3 target, -2 elsewhere
- 5-6: +5 target, -1 elsewhere

### 🎯 Debates (Rounds 4, 8, 12)
- All players choose 1 Debate card
- Roll 1d6 + Base Value
- Highest wins

**Base Values:**
- Knockout Blow: 3
- Steady Performance: 2
- Gaffe Recovery: 1

---

## CAMPAIGN POINTS

### Gaining:
- +3 per turn (base)
- +5 per turn in Rounds 10-12
- Fundraising cards
- No maximum (save across turns)

### Costs:
- Policy cards: 1-3 points
- Campaign cards: 0-3 points
- Must pay full cost

---

## SPECIAL ROUNDS

### Round 4, 8, 12: DEBATES
- All players participate
- Choose Debate card + roll dice
- Highest total wins

### Round 6: WILD CARDS ENTER
- Shuffle 3 Wild Cards into each deck

### Rounds 10-12: FINAL STRETCH
- Gain 5 Campaign Points per turn (not 3)
- "Final rounds only" cards can be played
- No new Events drawn

## COMMON COMBOS

### Card Synergies:
- **Infrastructure + Union Support** = +1 bonus
- **Mental Health + Education card** = +1 bonus

### Counters & Blocks:
- **Crisis Management** → Blocks Attack Ads/scandals
- **Veterans Benefits** → Neutralizes Military Scandal
- **Door-to-Door** → Immune to Attack Ads this turn

## STRATEGY CHEAT SHEET

### Early Game (Rounds 1-4)
- Secure safe states leaning your way
- Build Campaign Points (fundraising)
- Plant Rally markers
- Prepare for first Debate

### Mid Game (Rounds 5-8)
- Fight for swing states (PA, MI, WI, AZ, GA, NC, NV)
- Respond to Events
- Use Attack Ads on opponent's key states
- Wild Cards enter at Round 6

### Late Game (Rounds 9-12)
- **Count to 270** constantly
- Focus only on states needed for victory
- Use GOTV and Hail Mary
- Final Debate (Round 12) can swing everything
- Don't waste resources on won states

---

## COMMON MISTAKES

❌ Over-investing in already-won states
❌ Ignoring base states while chasing swing states
❌ Not adapting to Events
❌ Forgetting to roll Event severity
❌ Playing too defensively (you need 270!)
❌ Not counting opponent's path to 270

## VICTORY CHECKLIST

Before declaring victory, verify:
- ✅ You control states with lean ≥ +7
- ✅ Total electoral votes ≥ 270
- ✅ You've accounted for contested states (lean -6 to +6)

**Tiebreaker (if Round 12 ends tied):**
1. Most states controlled
2. Most swing states controlled
3. Highest Campaign Points remaining

## FUNDRAISING POOL

*Shared by all players - play once per turn*

- **Major Donor Event**: Gain 4 Campaign Points next turn
- **Small Dollar Donations**: Gain 2 Campaign Points + 1 lean
- **Super PAC Support**: Gain 5 Campaign Points, -1 lean in progressive states
- **Email Blast**: Gain 1 point per state you lead

---

## RALLY MARKERS

- Placed by "Grassroots Rally" card
- Grant +1 lean per turn for 3 turns
- Max 1 Rally marker per state
- Track duration with tokens

---

## WHEN TO USE WILD CARDS

**October Surprise** (Rounds 11-12):
- Play any Event from discard pile
- Use to trigger beneficial Event or repeat crisis for opponent

**Momentum Shift**:
- Swap lean of any 2 states
- Great for stealing opponent's state + securing your own

**Media Blackout**:
- Block all Attack Ads this round
- Use when opponent is about to attack
